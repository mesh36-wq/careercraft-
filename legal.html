Privacy Policy

Your CV. The CV builder stores your CV only in your browser (localStorage) on your own device. It is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so export a backup if you want to keep a copy.

Your account. If you create an account, we store your name, email address, registration date and verification status with our authentication provider, Supabase. Passwords are handled by the provider and are never visible to us or to administrators. Administrators can see account details only, never your CV.

Email. We send emails only for account verification and password reset.

Fonts. Pages load the Inter and Manrope fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load. No analytics or advertising tools are installed.
