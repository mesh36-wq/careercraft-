How do I download my CV as a PDF?
  1. Open the CV Builder and fill in your details.
  2. Tap Download PDF.
  3. In the print window, choose Save as PDF as the destination.
  4. Set paper size to A4 and margins to Default.
  5. Turn off Headers and footers if your browser shows that option.
  6. Tap Save or Download.
Where is my CV saved?

Only in this browser, on this device. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser or phone means your CV will not be there. Use Export backup in the Review step to keep a copy.

How do I move my CV to another device?

On the first device, go to Review and Download and tap Export backup. Send the file to yourself. On the other device, open the CV Builder and tap Import backup. Imported CVs are added as new CVs and never overwrite existing ones.

Can I keep more than one CV?

Yes. Use the My CVs menu at the top of the editor to create a new CV, or duplicate an existing one from the Review step to make a version for a specific job.

Are the writing tips AI?

No. They are simple checks that run in your browser, such as flagging weak phrases and suggesting action verbs. No AI service is connected and your text is not sent anywhere.

Will my CV pass employer screening systems?

We cannot promise that. Single-column templates are generally easier for software to read, but every employer's system is different. Use standard headings, plain text and keep your details accurate.

Writing tips

Start each point with an action verb. Describe results, not only duties. Add real numbers where you have them, and never invent facts. Keep your CV to one or two pages.
